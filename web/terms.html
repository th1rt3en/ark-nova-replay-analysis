Terms and privacy

A short, plain description of what this site keeps about you. It is a free fan project, not affiliated with Board Game Arena or the publisher of Ark Nova.

What an account is

An account is a username and a password. There is no email address and no real name. If your username matches a Board Game Arena player, you can copy that player's Elo as your starting rating once; this is not verified, so please only pick your own.

What is stored

  • Your username, the id of your account (a Board Game Arena player id or a number like P12) and the date you joined.
  • A hash of your password and of your recovery code. The password itself is never stored or logged.
  • Your rating, the rated games you played and the rating changes of each.
  • Your results in the mini games, and a random id of your browser (kept in your browser) for results given without an account.
  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in for 30 days after your last visit. It is used for nothing else.

Rated and friendly live games are recorded so that they can be replayed; the record holds the names the players used.

Who can see what

Your username, rating, rated games and mini game scores are public: they appear on your profile and on the leaderboards. Nothing else about you is shown.

Recovering and deleting

There is no email, so a lost password can only be reset with the recovery code shown when you signed up. Keep it somewhere safe. You can delete your account from your profile page: your name, password and sessions are removed and the account becomes "Deleted player"; the games and rating changes it took part in stay, because they are part of other players' history.

Fair play

Do not use several accounts to move your own rating, and do not pick a username that belongs to somebody else. Accounts that do may be removed.
